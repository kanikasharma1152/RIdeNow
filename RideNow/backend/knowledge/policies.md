# RideNow — Platform Policies and Guidelines

## 1. Cancellation Policy
### Passenger Cancellation
- **Free Cancellation Window:** A passenger may cancel a ride free of charge at any time while the status is `REQUESTED` (before a driver accepts) or within **2 minutes** after a driver is matched.
- **Post-Acceptance Cancellation:** If a passenger cancels more than 2 minutes after a driver has been matched and is actively en route, a cancellation fee of **₹30.00** may be billed to compensate the driver for fuel and time.
- **In-Progress Trips:** A ride that has already `STARTED` cannot be cancelled directly through the app. The driver must conclude the trip at a safe stopping point.

### Driver Rejection & Cancellation
- Drivers may decline an assigned ride if they encounter an emergency, mechanical trouble, or safety hazard.
- When a driver rejects a ride, the platform automatically re-assigns the passenger to the next nearest driver immediately.
- Excessive consecutive rejections by a driver (more than 3 within an hour) will temporarily place the driver in offline status to preserve network efficiency.

---

## 2. Refund and Dispute Policy
### When are refunds issued?
- In cases of incorrect route charging, billing discrepancies, or rides terminated prematurely due to driver fault, passengers can open a dispute.
- Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

### Fare Disputes
- If you believe you were overcharged due to an inaccurate distance calculation, contact support through the in-app AI Assistant or support desk with your Ride ID.

---

## 3. Code of Conduct
### Respect and Community Standards
- Passengers and drivers are expected to treat each other with courtesy and mutual respect.
- Discriminatory behavior, harassment, physical aggression, or vulgar language results in immediate suspension.
- Smoking, consumption of alcohol, or use of illegal substances inside vehicles is strictly prohibited.

### Vehicle Care
- Passengers must avoid damaging or dirtying the driver's vehicle. Cleaning fees of up to ₹500 may be levied for intentional spills or vehicle mess.
