# RideNow — Driver Partner Handbook

## 1. Getting Started as a Driver
### Going Online and Accepting Rides
1. Open the RideNow Driver Dashboard.
2. Ensure your GPS location permissions are enabled.
3. Toggle your status to **"Online"** using the switch in the header.
4. When online, RideNow matches nearby passenger requests automatically to your vehicle.
5. Review the pickup location, destination, and distance before choosing to accept or decline.

### Trip Lifecycle Execution
- **Step 1 — Ride Assigned:** You receive a real-time notification with passenger pickup coordinates.
- **Step 2 — En Route to Pickup:** Drive towards the pickup location. You can view the route on the Leaflet map.
- **Step 3 — Start Ride:** Once the passenger is safely seated inside, click **"Start Ride"**. The status changes to `STARTED`.
- **Step 4 — Complete Ride:** Upon reaching the destination, click **"Complete Ride"**. The system computes the final fare (`₹50 + distance × ₹12`).
- **Step 5 — Collect Payment:** Collect the displayed fare from the passenger and confirm payment.

## 2. Earnings and Performance
### How driver earnings work
- Drivers retain **85%** of the ride fare. The remaining 15% covers platform operations and insurance.
- Earnings from all completed rides are calculated and displayed on your **"Earnings"** tab (`/driver/earnings`).
- You can filter earnings by today, this week, this month, or custom date ranges.

### Driver Ratings and Standards
- Passengers rate each completed trip on a scale of 1 to 5 stars.
- Maintaining an average rating above **4.6 stars** qualifies drivers for top-tier bonuses and priority matching during peak hours.
