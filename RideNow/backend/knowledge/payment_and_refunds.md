# RideNow Payment & Refund Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
RideNow supports seamless cashless and digital payments:
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)
- Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay)
- Net Banking across all major banks
- Cash to Driver (where enabled by driver)

## Automatic Invoicing & GST
- GST invoices are generated immediately upon trip completion.
- Riders can download ride invoices directly from the Ride History section or request one via the Nova AI Assistant.
- Every invoice contains: Ride ID, pickup/dropoff points, distance breakdown, base fare, per km charge, and applicable tax.

## Refund Policy & Dispute Resolution
- **Incorrect Fare Charged:** If the route taken was excessively longer due to driver error or GPS drift, riders can request a fare review within 48 hours of trip completion.
- **Double Charges:** Any accidental duplicate payment authorization is automatically reversed to the source account within 3 to 5 business days.
- **Cancelled Ride Deductions:** If a cancellation fee is wrongly charged when the driver did not move towards pickup for over 10 minutes, the fee is fully refunded as RideNow platform credits or back to the source payment method.
- **Dispute Filing:** You can ask Nova AI: "Request a refund for trip #123" or file through Support.
