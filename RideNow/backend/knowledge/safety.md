# RideNow — Safety and Emergency Protocols

## 1. Safety Measures
### Driver Verification
All RideNow driver partners undergo strict verification before onboarding:
- Valid commercial driving license check.
- Vehicle registration certification (RC) and insurance verification.
- Routine vehicle safety and roadworthiness inspections.

### Live GPS Tracking & Ride Monitoring
- Every trip is tracked in real-time via high-precision GPS coordinates.
- Passengers and drivers can observe the vehicle's position, heading, and route progression continuously on the interactive map.

---

## 2. Emergency and Incident Assistance
### In-Ride Emergency
If you are involved in an emergency, accident, or feel unsafe:
1. Immediately contact local emergency law enforcement: **Dial 112 / 100**.
2. Alert the RideNow 24/7 Safety Response Team via the in-app AI Assistant by typing "Emergency" or "Help".
3. Provide your current location, vehicle plate number, and Ride ID.

### Medical Emergencies
In the event of medical distress during a ride, drivers are instructed to pull over at the nearest secure location and call emergency medical services (**108 / 102**).

---

## 3. Lost and Found Policy
### Item left in vehicle
If you forgot a personal belonging (phone, bag, keys) in a RideNow car:
1. Locate your trip under "Ride History" in the app.
2. Note your Ride ID and the driver details.
3. Message the AI Assistant or customer support with a detailed description of the lost item.
4. The support team will facilitate contact with your driver to arrange a safe return.
