# RideNow AI Assistant (Nova) Usage Guide

## Who is Nova?
Nova is RideNow's intelligent in-app mobility assistant. Powered by Groq's ultra-low latency Llama-3.3-70B model, Nova provides instantaneous answers and performs platform actions on your behalf with complete security and role boundaries.

## What Can Nova Do for Passengers?
1. **Real-Time Ride Tracking:** Ask "Where is my ride?" or "Show my active driver" to view current status and pickup/dropoff points.
2. **Transparent Fare Estimates:** Inquire "How much for 15 km?" or "Fare to airport" to get transparent breakdowns of base fare and per-kilometer rates.
3. **Trip History & Invoices:** Ask "Show my recent trips" or "What did I spend on my last ride?"
4. **Cancellation Assistance:** Request "Cancel my ride". Nova will verify active rides and confirm cancellation with clear terms.
5. **Knowledge Base Search:** Ask any question regarding platform policies, cancellation fees, lost items, or safety protocols.

## What Can Nova Do for Drivers?
1. **Operational Summaries:** "What are my earnings today?" gives today's, this week's, and this month's gross earnings and completed trip counts.
2. **Shift Briefings:** Drivers receive hotspot advice, surge zones, and ETA optimizations to maximize their hourly earnings.
3. **Driver Guidelines:** Instant queries on vehicle requirements, rating preservation, and payout schedules.

## Privacy & Security Guarantees
- Nova only accesses your own personal ride and profile data.
- AI operations are sandboxed through strict API tool layers—the AI never executes direct database queries or modifies financial transactions arbitrarily.
