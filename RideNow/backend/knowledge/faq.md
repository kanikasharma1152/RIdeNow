# RideNow — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. Booking a Ride
### How do I book a ride?
1. Log in to your RideNow passenger account.
2. Go to the "Book a Ride" tab.
3. Enter your pickup and dropoff locations.
4. Review the estimated distance, fare, and route on the live map.
5. Click "Confirm & Request Ride". The system will automatically locate and match the nearest available driver.

### How does driver matching work?
RideNow uses an intelligent Haversine distance algorithm combined with live driver coordinates. Only drivers who are currently online and available are eligible. If a driver declines or is unreachable, RideNow automatically re-assigns the ride to the next closest driver without cancelling your request.

### Can I book a ride in advance?
Currently, RideNow provides instant on-demand bookings. Scheduled rides are part of our upcoming roadmap.

## 2. Pricing and Fares
### How is my ride fare calculated?
RideNow uses a transparent, fixed-rate pricing model:
- **Base Fare:** ₹50.00
- **Per Kilometer Rate:** ₹12.00 per km
- **Formula:** `Total Fare = ₹50 + (Distance in km × ₹12)`
The fare is calculated accurately based on the actual road distance measured via high-precision routing.

### Are there surge or hidden charges?
No. RideNow does not charge hidden platform fees. Any tolls or parking charges during a ride are paid directly by the passenger unless specified.

---

## 3. Payments
### What payment methods are supported?
RideNow currently supports Cash on delivery (Cash to driver) and direct digital checkout. Digital wallets and card payments can be settled securely.

### When do I pay for my ride?
Payment is due upon arrival at your dropoff location when the driver completes the ride.

---

## 4. Account and Profiles
### Can I be both a passenger and a driver?
Each RideNow account has a designated role (`passenger` or `driver`). To drive with RideNow, you can register a driver profile with your driving license, vehicle number, vehicle model, and vehicle type.

### How do I update my profile details?
Navigate to the "Profile" section in your sidebar menu. You can update your name, email address, and phone number. Drivers can also update vehicle details.
