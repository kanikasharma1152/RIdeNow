# Emergency Guidelines & Crisis Protocols

## Immediate Life-Threatening Emergencies
If you, your passenger, or anyone around you is in immediate physical danger, medical crisis, or severe accident:
1. Always dial national emergency services directly from your phone (112 / 100 in India, 911 in the US).
2. Move to a well-lit, public location if safe to do so.
3. Use the RideNow in-app SOS button to record an operational critical safety event, alerting the platform operations monitoring center.
4. Note: The RideNow platform provides automated telemetry monitoring and incident logging, but cannot replace direct official emergency dispatcher contact.

## RideNow SOS Trigger Workflow
- Activating Emergency SOS creates an immediate CRITICAL safety record tied to your account and active trip.
- Your designated trusted emergency contacts can be shared your live trip status.
- The command center team receives an urgent incident notification.
- To deactivate a false alarm, use the Resolve button or notify safety support.

## Vehicle Breakdown or Minor Collision
- Ensure the vehicle is shifted to the road shoulder or safety bay.
- Turn on emergency hazard lights.
- In-app assistance: Report a safety concern under "Vehicle Issue" or cancel the trip using standard safety cancellation.
