# Passenger Safety Standards & Corridor Protection

## Pre-Boarding Verification Checklist
Before entering any RideNow vehicle:
1. Verify the license plate number displayed in your app matches the vehicle.
2. Confirm the vehicle model and color with the digital ride card.
3. Check the driver's first name and ask "Who are you picking up?" to ensure mutual identity verification.
4. Never board a vehicle whose license plate differs from your confirmed dispatch.

## Real-Time Trip Corridor Monitoring
RideNow actively monitors ongoing trips:
- Route Deviation Shield: Our GPS engine calculates the expected corridor and flags unexpected diversions.
- Unplanned Stops: Extended stationary stops exceeding 5 minutes trigger an automated safety check.
- Live Driver Telemetry: You can verify your driver's live GPS coordinates directly inside the app at any moment.

## Sharing Trips with Trusted Contacts
- You can add up to 5 family members or trusted contacts in the Safety Command Center.
- One-click trip sharing creates an expiring, privacy-preserving tracking link.
- Trusted contacts do not need an account or login to view your route and current progress.
