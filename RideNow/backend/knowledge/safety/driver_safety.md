# Driver Safety Protocols & Partner Guidelines

## Captain Emergency Assistance (Driver SOS)
RideNow empowers drivers with comprehensive on-road safety:
- If you face abusive behavior, threats, medical duress, or road danger, use the Driver SOS feature in your Driver Dashboard.
- Driver SOS registers a CRITICAL platform incident and alerts operations.
- If in physical danger, pull over in a safe, populated area and call local emergency services immediately (dial 112).

## Unruly or Intoxicated Passengers
- Maintain a calm, professional tone at all times.
- If a passenger refuses to comply with basic safety rules or becomes aggressive, safely terminate the trip at a secure, well-lit location.
- Immediately file a report under "Passenger Behavior Concern" in the Safety Center to protect your driver rating and platform standing.

## Route Navigation and Detours
- Traffic congestion or construction closures may require detours.
- Inform the passenger politely when taking an alternative route: "There is roadwork ahead, so I am taking an alternate route."
- This helps avoid passenger confusion and prevents unnecessary route deviation alerts.
