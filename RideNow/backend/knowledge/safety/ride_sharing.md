# Ride Sharing & Live Trip Tracking Links

## Privacy-Preserving Trip Sharing
RideNow allows passengers to share their live journey with friends and family:
- Share links use cryptographically random 256-bit expiring tokens.
- No personal passwords, payment methods, full home addresses, or phone numbers are exposed.
- Viewers can monitor vehicle model, license plate, driver first name, live location, and destination.

## Expiration and Revocation
- Shared links automatically expire 24 hours after generation.
- Passengers can revoke a shared link at any time from the Safety Center or active ride panel.
- Once a trip is completed or cancelled, the live tracking automatically transitions to final trip summary.

## Browser-Based Sharing
- If direct messaging (SMS/WhatsApp) is not configured on the device, RideNow generates a formatted trip text that can be copied with one click and pasted into any messaging app.
