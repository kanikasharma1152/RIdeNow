# Project Plan

## Phase 1 — Foundation
Git/GitHub, React/Vite, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, environment setup.

## Phase 2 — Authentication
Registration, login, password hashing, JWT, protected routes and Rider/Driver/Admin roles.

## Phase 3 — Users & Drivers
Profiles, vehicles, driver verification and online/offline status.

## Phase 4 — Ride MVP
Ride request, nearby-driver search, accept/reject, start/end ride and ride history.

Ride states:
REQUESTED → ACCEPTED → ARRIVING → STARTED → COMPLETED

## Phase 5 — Maps & Fare
Pickup/drop, route, distance, ETA, base fare, distance fare, time fare and demand multiplier.

## Phase 6 — Realtime
WebSockets, live driver location, ride events and Redis.

## Phase 7 — Payments & Notifications
Payment flow, receipt, success/failure handling and ride notifications.

## Phase 8 — Ratings & Admin
Ratings, reviews, admin dashboard, revenue and ride analytics.

## Phase 9 — AI
ETA, demand, fare prediction, driver recommendation and anomaly detection.

## Phase 10 — Production
Testing, logging, Docker, CI/CD basics, deployment and documentation.
